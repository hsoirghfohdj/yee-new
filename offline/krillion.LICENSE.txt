Krillion (one-file edition) is built from OpenKrillion:
https://github.com/VulcanEon/openkrillion.com-service

The game code, pixel art, sounds and sample questions are OpenKrillion's, used under the MIT
license below. Changes for this edition: it runs in the browser (the Cloudflare server is replaced
by a small local stand-in that keeps runs in browser storage), and the website-only parts
(analytics, feedback forms, privacy and AI-docs pages) are left out.

Like openkrillion.com, the daily prompts and accepted answers are the public reference puzzle from
krillion.io, fetched each day (through api/krillion.js on Classboard's Vercel site). That puzzle
data is not part of this repository and is not covered by the MIT license. Not affiliated with
krillion.io.
