How to play Krillion

How a dive works

Each dive has seven prompts. You get three seconds to read each one, then twenty-five seconds to type a single answer. Any answer that fits the category counts, but the less obvious it is, the more points it is worth and the deeper it sinks. If the clock runs out with text in the box, that answer is checked for you; an empty box scores zero.

The six rarity tiers

Plankton is worth 10 points: the answer everyone blurts out. Too Clever is 15: the “obscure” answer the whole class thought of. Schooler is 30, Rare is 60, Deep Cut is 85, and One in a Krillion is 100: a single hand-picked gem in each category. An answer that is not on the list does not score; you can try again until the clock runs out.

Spelling and corrections

Capital letters, spaces, punctuation and accents do not matter. Small typos and common short forms (like “fries” for French fries or “H” for hydrogen) are matched to the right answer, and you are shown the correction first: press DIVE again to lock it in, or edit it. You can turn the preview off in Settings.

Today’s puzzle

The prompts are the daily Krillion puzzle, the same seven that krillion.io and openkrillion.com use, loaded fresh each day along with every accepted answer. It changes at midnight New York time (9 PM on the West Coast). You get one daily dive; a practice dive replays the same prompts as often as you like and does not count toward your streak. If the puzzle can’t be reached, you get OpenKrillion’s sample questions instead, and the title screen says so.

Depth, logbook and dive buddies

Every point sinks you ten metres, so a perfect 700 reaches 7,000 m. Your logbook keeps your daily scores and streak. Long daily streaks unlock new dive buddies in the wardrobe: 5 days, 14, 30 and 45. After a dive, tap any round to see every accepted answer.

About this version

This is a one-file edition of OpenKrillion, an open-source game made by the OpenKrillion contributors under the MIT license. The game runs in your browser; only the day’s puzzle is fetched, the same way openkrillion.com gets it. It is independent of, and not affiliated with, the original Krillion at krillion.io.
