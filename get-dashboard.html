Class Dash

A dashboard for Canvas: grades, to-dos, a calendar and announcements, with built-in games. It lives in one bookmark.

🎓 Class Dash
  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B (Mac: ⌘+Shift+B).
  2. Drag the blue button onto the bookmarks bar.
  3. Open your school's Canvas, log in, and click the bookmark. Click it again or press Esc to close it.

Can't drag it? Copy it instead

Create a new bookmark, name it "Class Dash", and paste this into the URL field.

What's inside

  • Overview: average grade, estimated GPA, what's due this week, overdue work, announcements and recent grades
  • To-Do: everything due, grouped by overdue, today, this week and later. Checking an item off only hides it here.
  • Grades and Classes: click any class to see every assignment with its score and quick links
  • Calendar: 3 weeks of due dates, colored by class
  • Classic games: A Dark Room, 2048, Hextris, T-Rex Runner, Tetris and Clumsy Bird. These are real open-source games from GitHub, packed into the bookmark with their licenses.
  • Quick games: Snake, Brick Breaker, Meteor Dodge, Memory Match and Mines

Click the blue button on this page to try the games. The Canvas parts only load on your Canvas site.
